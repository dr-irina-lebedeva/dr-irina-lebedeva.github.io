Projects

Dataset · Benchmark · Model
MEBeauty: Human Aesthetic Preference Dataset & Benchmark

A multi-ethnic facial beauty dataset collected in the wild, with human attractiveness ratings, first published in 2022 and now fully open and reproducible.

  • Dataset: about 2,500 faces from six ethnic groups, of different genders and ages, cleaned, with person-level splits and an improved label.
  • Benchmark: 21 methods, from 2006 to today's foundation models, including my own approach, all under one protocol.
  • Reference model: a DINOv2-based model, ready to use in two lines of Python, with its accuracy across ethnic groups reported openly.
  • Publication: the original peer-reviewed paper, "MEBeauty: A Multi-Ethnic Facial Beauty Dataset in the Wild", Neural Computing & Applications, 2022.

For non-commercial academic research only.
